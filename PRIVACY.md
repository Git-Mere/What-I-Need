# Privacy Policy for "What I Need"

_Last updated: 2026-07-05_

"What I Need" (the "Extension") is a browser extension that provides an on-page
utility toolbar (multi-keyword search, drawing brush, memo, calculator, and
scroll-position markers) for use while browsing the web.

This policy explains how the Extension handles your data.

## Summary

**The Extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or
usage data.** It has no servers, makes no network requests, and includes no
analytics or tracking. Everything it stores stays on your own device.

## What data the Extension stores

To make its features work and remember your preferences between sessions, the
Extension saves the following locally on your device using the browser's
`chrome.storage.local` API:

- **Settings** – toolbar position and enabled state, and brush color and width.
- **Memo text** – any note you type into the memo tool.
- **Calculator history** – the calculations you perform in the calculator tool.

This information is created by you, for your own use, and is stored only on your
device.

## What the Extension does NOT do

- It does **not** send any data off your device.
- It does **not** make any network requests to any server.
- It does **not** collect personally identifiable information, browsing history,
  location, or authentication data.
- It does **not** use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- It does **not** sell or transfer any user data to third parties.
- It does **not** use data for creditworthiness or lending purposes.

## Permissions

- **`storage`** – used only to save the settings and content described above on
  your device.
- **Host access (`<all_urls>`)** – required so the toolbar can appear and its
  tools can work on whatever page you are currently viewing. The Extension only
  renders its own interface and acts locally on the current page; it does not
  read, collect, or transmit page content.

## Removing your data

All stored data lives in your browser. You can clear it at any time by removing
the Extension, or via your browser's extension/site data controls.
